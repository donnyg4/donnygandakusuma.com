My name is Donny Gandakusuma. I'm a freelance comic artist and illustrator based in Indonesia, working entirely in traditional black and white ink.

I've been drawing comics and illustrations for years, taking influence from classic American comic art — the bold lines, heavy shadows, and kinetic energy that made Batman, Conan, and Red Sonja icons of the medium. Everything I create is done by hand, with real ink on real paper. No digital shortcuts. No AI. Every line is deliberate.

My original artworks are collected by fans across the USA, Europe, and beyond. I've been selling on eBay since 2013, with 100% positive feedback from buyers who appreciate the care that goes into every piece — from the drawing itself to the packaging and shipping.

Beyond selling existing pieces, I'm available for commissions. Whether you want a favourite character captured in the classic comic style, or a fully original scene — I work closely with collectors to bring their vision to life.

Some of my comics have also been published. You can see selected published works in the Portfolio section.
